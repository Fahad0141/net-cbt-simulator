# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, welcome newcomers (many contributors are students
preparing for their own tests), and keep discussions focused on improving the simulator and its questions.
Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a confidential issue to the maintainers or contacting them through the
repository's listed contact. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that violate this code
and may temporarily or permanently ban repeat offenders.
